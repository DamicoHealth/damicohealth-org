Educating patients and providers

Education is one of the highest-impact things we do. In the communities we serve, the person managing a patient is often a nurse or a village-level provider working with limited training and few resources. Strengthening their knowledge improves care for far more patients than any single clinician can reach directly.

We teach in two directions. We educate patients, giving them practical, culturally relevant health information they can act on with the resources they actually have. And, more importantly, we educate the local healthcare providers who deliver care every day, addressing real gaps in clinical knowledge with hands-on, practical training.

This work happens year-round through our in-country team led by Dr. Gloria Wafula, and directly during our visits. By investing in local providers rather than working around them, we help build a healthcare system that grows stronger over time.
