Your money makes a difference. Here is our pledge.

At Damico Health, we understand that every dollar you donate is a personal sacrifice, an act of generosity driven by a desire to make a meaningful impact on global healthcare. We are committed to stewarding your gift with integrity, using every dollar faithfully and ethically to its fullest purpose. Below is a clear breakdown of where your support goes, and where it does not.

Your donation supports

Acute and surgical care
Life-changing surgery delivered with local hospital partners, including hernia repair to prevent bowel obstruction and corrective procedures that restore quality of life.
Surgical care provided in trusted local facilities, giving patients access to specialists and procedures usually out of reach.
Palliative care
Home visits, pain management, symptom control, and essential medications for patients facing serious or life-limiting illness.
Psychosocial support for patients and their caregivers, delivered year-round.
Women's health
Cervical cancer screening, family planning, and maternal health care.
Treatment for acute gynecologic conditions, with an emphasis on dignity and access.
Essential medications and medical supplies
Life-saving medications, diagnostic tests, lab consumables, wound care materials, and treatment kits.
Includes antibiotics, antiparasitics, antifungals, and essential STI testing.
Provider and patient education
Hands-on clinical training for the local nurses and providers who care for patients every day.
Practical, culturally relevant health education that patients can act on.
Critical infrastructure support
Power solutions (mobile power solutions, rechargeable batteries), water systems, and cold-chain logistics.
Includes lighting and equipment necessary to keep mobile and remote clinics functional and safe.

Your donation does not support

Administrative overhead
No funds are used for website hosting, email servers, social media, or internal IT tools.
All tech infrastructure is personally funded.
Government and legal compliance fees
Tax-exempt status maintenance, registrations, and legal filings are covered by our leadership, not donors.
Travel and lodging expenses
Flights, accommodations, meals, and transport for team members are self-funded.
Branding and promotional materials
T-shirts, stickers, banners, and business cards are not paid for with donated funds. They are covered personally.
Unmonitored partner organizations
We only fund partners with strict accountability, transparency, and measurable impact reporting.
Disproportionate or high-cost capital expenditures
We avoid large purchases that would divert funds from core services.
For example: all diagnostic ultrasound and imaging equipment has been purchased outright by our U.S.-based physicians to avoid burdening our operating funds.

We exist to serve, not spend. Every dollar is intentional, and every gift is honored with integrity. Thank you for trusting us.
