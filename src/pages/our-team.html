Our team

Damico Health is led by healthcare professionals who live and work in Uganda, with a permanent medical team that cares for patients all year.

{{photo id="gloria" alt="Portrait of Dr. Gloria Wafula in a white coat." sizes="(min-width: 900px) 30vw, 90vw" pos="50% 15%"}}

Dr. Gloria Wafula

Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Gloria leads our in-country team and our palliative care program. She is one of the few formally trained palliative care specialists in the region, and she directs the provider education we run throughout the year.

Palliative care

{{photo id="erhardt" alt="Portrait of Dr. Kansiime Erhardt in a light jacket." sizes="(min-width: 900px) 30vw, 90vw" pos="50% 15%"}}

Dr. Kansiime Erhardt

Chief of Surgery

Dr. Erhardt leads our surgical program, bringing experienced surgeons together with local hospitals so that patients can have surgery close to home.
