Year-round medical care for rural Uganda

Damico Health keeps a permanent medical team in Mukono District. We provide surgery, palliative care, women's health and ultrasound to patients who could not otherwise afford them, and we train the local providers who carry the work forward.

Donate See our work

Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.

Led by Ugandan physicians. Our team lives and works in the communities it serves.

All donations go to care. Overhead, travel and equipment are paid for by our leadership.

Most patients who delay care do so because of cost

In May 2026 we asked patients what had kept them from getting care sooner. Nearly three in four said cost. About one in five said distance.

People will travel for care they trust and can afford. So we put our work into removing the cost, and into bringing surgeons and specialists to local facilities that patients can reach.

A team that lives where it works

Our in-country team is led by Dr. Gloria Wafula, Chief Medical Officer, and Dr. Kansiime Erhardt, Chief of Surgery. They care for patients throughout the year and follow complex cases long after a first visit.

A free medical record for teams working without internet

Commercial record systems cost thousands of dollars a year and assume a constant connection. We built DH Field EMR for clinics that have neither, and we give it away to any organization doing similar work.
